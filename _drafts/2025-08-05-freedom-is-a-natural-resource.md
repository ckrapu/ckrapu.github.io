To America's urbanites, new immigrants, and everyone else 




----

The American frontier closed over 130 years but I still think about it all the time. 

I think about it when I look at our farm and see the spot where Dad said that the first settler on this land--an amputee veteran of the War Between the States from Pennsylvania--laid down his homestead.

It's with me when I honor the memory of Sonja, the woman who cared for me during my entire childhood. A member of the Greatest Generation and the daughter of Norwegian immigrants, she was infinitely steady and calm.

North Dakota has a special place in the story of the frontier because it was the last of the farm states to be settled. Illinois, Ohio, et al. were populated decades earlier.
